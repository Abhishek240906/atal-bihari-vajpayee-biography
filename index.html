ABOUT THE LEADER

Atal Bihari Vajpayee

Atal Bihari Vajpayee was born on 25 December 1924 in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh. He became one of India's most respected political leaders and was known for his powerful speeches, poetry and commitment to public life.

He served as the Prime Minister of India three times and became one of the most prominent leaders of the Bharatiya Janata Party.
